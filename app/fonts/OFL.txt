Vice Cover display font
=======================

The display face this app letters its captions in is:

  Noto Sans Display ExtraCondensed Black
  (family "Noto Sans Display", width ExtraCondensed, weight 900)

The file vice-display.woff2 beside this note is a subset of that face,
made for this app with fontTools: Latin letters, digits and punctuation
only, 190 glyphs. The full face is published by Google under the SIL Open
